I mean this movie was amazing. Jodie Foster and Anthony Hopkins... The actors you are. Hannibal Lecter was truly iconic, I couldn't help but love him, the crazy bastard. And Clarice was a wonderful main character. 

The exploration of her character through the probing of Dr Lecter, as a sort of payment for help with the Buffalo bill case, along with her growth in both dealing with the case and Hannibal was great. You were really placed in her head for a lot of those scenes, with Anthony Hopkins staring right into your soul.

Great great movie. I know it did very well in the awards, and is critically aclaimed, but I put it as underrated simply because I have not heard enough great things about it. Watch it.
