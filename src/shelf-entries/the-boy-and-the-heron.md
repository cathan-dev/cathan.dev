I just love a whimsical world full of mystery.

Of course, the animation is amazing, and the voice acting was great; but wow the world just sucked me in. The whole time I was trying to piece together the mysteries, and although I got some stuff right, by the end of the movie I was filled with questions about the movie. 

I feel kinda dumb cus I wasn't able to fully piece together the themes and such at the end, I just got hella goosebumps and a bit teary eyed. But still, very good!
