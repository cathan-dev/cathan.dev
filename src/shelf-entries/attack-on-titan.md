This was my first anime that I watched. I watched season 1 around when it was released and it changed my perspective on what an animated show could be. I went on to read it in it's entirety around the release of season 4, but recently I rewatched the whole show with my friend. 

It both solidified some of the things I love about the story, but I also found myself seeing more flaws in it than I thought I would. I still think it's a great anime, the world is amazing and the slow drip of information throughout is some of the best I've ever seen, but I wouldn't say it's anywhere near perfect. 

I still love you Attack on Titan.
